Why the gauge check reads under 9 mm. NMRA N is defined as 1 : 160, but the track it runs on is 9 mm between the rails. Reduce real standard gauge by 160 and you get 8.969 mm — so N track is very slightly wide for its own scale, which is the long-standing compromise the standard accepts. Dividing 1435 by 9 instead gives 1 : 159.44, offered above for anyone who wants the gauge to come out exact. For parts that sit alongside bought stock, stay on 160.

Exact inputs: 1 ft = 304.8 mm, 1 in = 25.4 mm. Standard gauge is defined as 1435 mm, while 4 ft 8½ in works out to 1435.1 mm — which is why the check above reads a thousandth over 9.
